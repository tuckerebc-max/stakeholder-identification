# Stakeholder taxonomy and prompts

Use this reference when the initial stakeholder list is thin or dominated by formal leadership.

## Coverage prompts

- Who decides, funds, approves, regulates, or can veto?
- Who implements, operates, supports, or supplies data?
- Who uses the result every day?
- Who bears cost, risk, workload, exclusion, or unintended consequences?
- Who is not represented by the official organization chart?
- Who can delay, reinterpret, amplify, challenge, or legally constrain the work?
- Who will be affected later even if they are not affected during launch?

## Stakeholder versus stakeholder group

Keep a group together when members share the same relationship, interest, power, and engagement need. Split it when members differ materially in impact, decision rights, access, risk, or likely position. Record a named person only when the source identifies them or current research verifies them.

## Engagement verbs

- **Inform:** provide timely, usable information; no promise of influence.
- **Consult:** request input and explain how it will be considered.
- **Involve:** include participants in problem framing or option development.
- **Collaborate:** share work and decision influence.
- **Negotiate:** address conflicting interests, commitments, or resources.
- **Approve:** obtain a formal decision or authorization.
- **Empower:** transfer meaningful decision authority to the affected group.

Choose the verb that matches actual authority. Do not call an announcement “engagement.”
